# Project Page and PRD outline

Use one evolving Page. Adapt the headings and length to the medium. The Page title should name the project and its purpose, without repeating it as the body's first heading. Ordinary text should remain native and editable.

Keep `PROJECT-KNOWLEDGE.md` in the active project root current alongside this Page. Record the real Page link, confirmed decisions, open assumptions, and later build/design evidence so Submit can recover context. Fresh participant Page edits should be reconciled into the summary, not overwritten from it.

Open with a short statement of the idea, whom it serves, and what experience or meaning it aims to create. Do not present agent suggestions as participant decisions.

## Project idea

What the participant hopes to make and its intended medium. Preserve the team's language and creative intent.

## Audience and motivations

A concise provisional persona: relevant demographics or circumstances, needs, behaviors, pain points, values, and why the experience might matter. Include only material supplied or agreed. Keep researched evidence distinct from assumptions.

## Context

When, where, and with whom the experience happens. Include day or night, indoors or outdoors, duration, or social conditions only when relevant or already discussed.

## Strategic pyramid

The confirmed image and a short editable explanation. Initial state: what, who, why; when and where; how to explore. After reference discussion, update how to the agreed approach or expression. Verify that the image and text agree.

## Related experiences

A few direct source links with the aspects the team wants to borrow, adapt, or avoid. Explain reference relevance; do not claim these projects validate audience demand.

For apps, websites, and webapps, include the related competitor findings from Mobbin alongside official store listings, product websites, and other actual research. Distinguish direct competitors, adjacent products, and interaction inspiration. Attribute interface observations to the inspected Mobbin captures and broader product claims to the relevant sources; mark missing evidence and pending research.

## Intended approach

The distinctive quality, creative belief, message, or chosen way to deliver the experience. Differentiation is optional. Record any selected reference aspects.

## Primary user flows

For apps, websites, and webapps, record the team's explicitly agreed primary journey and any essential related paths: relevant role, starting situation and intended outcome, ordered user actions and screens or states, and essential feedback. Include the actual Mobbin flow links and patterns adapted for this project. Mark material assumptions and pending research clearly; proposed flows remain suggestions until agreed. Omit this section for other media unless the team finds it useful. Develop uses the confirmed flows to plan the prototype; Design preserves them while redesigning the greybox or wireframes.

## Hackathon scope

The agreed capabilities or experience elements to make. Include priorities only if a prioritization discussion was needed. Record explicit exclusions or deferred ideas when the team has decided them. Include relevant time, people, equipment, materials, and access constraints.

## Prototype suggestion

Briefly suggest the appropriate demonstration based on their idea: interactive flow, playable scene, spatial mockup, rehearsal, or another fitting form. Identify whether it is suggested or confirmed. Develop can refine this after reading the team's edits.

## Open questions and assumptions

Only uncertainties that materially affect the build or cultural interpretation. Do not invent demographic certainty, impact metrics, research results, or permissions. A short list is enough; omit it when there is nothing material.

## Build and experience review

Develop adds the agreed plan, actual prototype location, working behavior, simulations, participant feedback, and next changes here. Keep technical detail proportional to what the team needs.

## Design direction

Design adds source references, the agreed mood and visual or sensory decisions, actual samples, and the selected direction here. For digital projects, record Mobbin redesign targets, source links, selected changes, actual before/after results where available, and remaining limitations.

## Workshop progress

Current stage; confirmed decisions; unresolved questions; next checkpoint. Keep this concise. Later stages read fresh Page content rather than relying on this progress note alone.

## Submission

Submit adds the seven-section Common Ground entry here when requested. Preserve the project strategy and PRD. Keep incomplete-field notes outside the reusable submission and record the latest draft, section word counts, and review status in the knowledge base.
