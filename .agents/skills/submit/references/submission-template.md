# Common Ground — Project Submission Template

Please submit one entry per team by the submission deadline. Keep your descriptions concise and make sure your project is easy to understand and try out!

## 1. Team Details

— Project name:

— Team name:

— Team members: Names of all team members

— Tracks: Select up to TWO of the four available tracks:

- Design × Tech
- Culture × Tech
- Best Use of Tech
- Common Ground Award

## 2. Project Overview (Max. 100 words)

— One-liner: Describe your project in one sentence.

— The problem or inspiration: What sparked this idea? What are you trying to explore, solve or reimagine?

— The solution: What did you build, and how does it work?

— What makes it interesting? What is original, unexpected or meaningful about your approach?

## 3. The Build (Max. 100 words)

— Technology used: List the tools, technologies, hardware, software or AI models used.

— What did you make? Briefly describe what works right now. Is it a prototype, an interactive demo or a functioning MVP?

— What did you experiment with? What did you try, learn or discover during the build?

— What would you do next? If you had more time, what would you improve or explore?

## 4. Project Links & Demo

Please include these following sources:

— Live demo / deployed project:

— Demo video: Especially useful if the project requires hardware or cannot be accessed remotely.

— GitHub / source code (required unless the project isn't based on code):

— Figma / prototype link (optional):

— Other supporting materials: Images, documentation or relevant links.

Make sure links are accessible to the judges. If your project requires a specific setup, include brief instructions on how to try it.

## 5. Track-Specific Reflection

For Design × Tech:

How does your project push interaction, interfaces or the relationship between people and technology in a new direction?

For Culture × Tech:

How does your project meaningfully engage with, reinterpret or preserve the cultural subject it explores?

If submitting to both tracks, answer both questions.

## 6. Team & Collaboration

How did your team bring different skills, disciplines or perspectives together? What did you contribute or discover by working together?

## 7. AI & Tool Attribution

Briefly list any AI tools, models or external resources used, and what you used them for. Credit any third-party assets, datasets or references where applicable.

A final note: Don't worry about having the most polished or technically complex project. We want to understand your idea, what you explored, what you built in a day, and why it matters.
